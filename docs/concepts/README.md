# Concepts

Read these to understand how ai-operator works, without reading the code:
how a milestone becomes merge requests, the roles of the agents (planner,
implementer, reviewer, pilot, scout), what the model can and cannot reach,
and a glossary of the terms the other pages use.

## Pages

In reading order:

1. **[How it works](how-it-works.md)**: the flow from a milestone spec to
   merged and released work, on autopilot or by hand, with the pods and the
   Desk.
2. **[Roles](roles.md)**: the planner, implementer, reviewers, describer and
   pilot, and the helpers. What each does, when it runs and what you see of
   it.
3. **[Security](security.md)**: what the model can and cannot reach, what
   the Desk login protects and where your secrets live.
4. **[Glossary](glossary.md)**: every term the docs use, one or two
   sentences each.
